Where things stand

PNG and Australia signed a defence treaty in late 2025 that opens a path for PNG citizens to join the ADF. Right now, only PNG citizens who already hold Australian permanent residency can apply — that started 1 January 2026. A wider pathway for PNG-based citizens is still being worked out.

  • It's a genuinely new process, so details are still changing.
  • It won't be automatic — you'll need to meet the same standards as any ADF applicant.
  • Confirm anything important with ADF Careers or the PNG Department of Defence before acting on it.

What the selection process usually looks like
  1. Application & eligibility checkCitizenship/residency, age, education, and character are reviewed first.
  2. Aptitude testNumeracy, literacy, and reasoning — helps match you to a suitable role.
  3. Medical assessmentHealth history, vision, hearing, dental, and mental health screening.
  4. Fitness testPush-ups, sit-ups, and a beep test or shuttle run.
  5. InterviewA conversation about your motivation and fit for the role.
  6. Background checksPolice and reference checks, likely in both countries for this pathway.
  7. Offer & enlistmentYour role and training location are confirmed.

Where to check for updates

Keep an eye on the PNG Department of Defence, the Australian Department of Defence, and ADF Careers' official site. Be wary of any site claiming to have an application form for this pathway — as of mid-2026 there's no confirmed direct application process yet.
