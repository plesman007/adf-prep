Where things stand

PNG and Australia signed a defence treaty in late 2025 that opens a path for PNG citizens to join the ADF. Right now, only PNG citizens who already hold Australian permanent residency can apply — that started 1 January 2026. A wider pathway for PNG-based citizens is still being worked out.

  • It's a genuinely new process, so details are still changing.
  • It won't be automatic — you'll need to meet the same standards as any ADF applicant.
  • Confirm anything important with ADF Careers or the PNG Department of Defence before acting on it.

How to join, step by step

The simple version — no jargon, just what happens and roughly what it involves.

  1. 1
    Check you're eligibleRight now that mainly means holding Australian permanent residency as a PNG citizen. Confirm your own situation with ADF Careers.
  2. 2
    ApplySubmit your application when the pathway is open to you, with your citizenship, education, and character details.
  3. 3
    Sit the aptitude testVerbal, numerical, and abstract reasoning questions under a strict time limit. Practice in the Aptitude test tab.
  4. 4
    Pass the medical checkA full health, vision, hearing, dental, and mental health review against ADF standards.
  5. 5
    Pass the fitness testPush-ups, sit-ups, and a beep test or shuttle run. Track your training in the Fitness tab.
  6. 6
    Attend an interviewA conversation about why you want to join and whether a role is a good fit for you.
  7. 7
    Background checksPolice and reference checks — likely across both PNG and Australia for this pathway.
  8. 8
    Get your offerIf you're successful, your role and training location are confirmed and enlistment begins.
Stay updated through the PNG Department of Defence, the Australian Department of Defence, and ADF Careers' official site. Be wary of any site claiming to have an application form for this pathway — as of mid-2026 there's no confirmed direct process yet.
