Where the pathway stands

Under the PNG–Australia Mutual Defence Treaty (the "Pukpuk Treaty"), signed in late 2025, Australia and PNG agreed to build a recruitment pathway allowing PNG citizens to enlist in the Australian Defence Force. From 1 January 2026, the first phase opened applications only to PNG citizens who already hold Australian permanent residency. A bilateral taskforce is still finalising the broader pathway that would let PNG-based citizens apply directly — PNG's government has said this will not be automatic entry, and applicants will need to meet the same standards as any other ADF candidate.

This is a genuinely new and still-moving process. Treat every date and detail here as a planning guide, not an official announcement — confirm current status with ADF Careers, the PNG Department of Defence, or PNG media before you act on it.

Typical ADF selection stages

  1. Application & eligibility checkCitizenship/residency status, age, education level, and character requirements are reviewed first.
  2. Aptitude testAssesses numeracy, literacy, reasoning, and mechanical comprehension to help match you to suitable roles.
  3. Medical assessmentFull health history, vision, hearing, dental, and mental health screening against role-specific medical standards.
  4. Physical Fitness Assessment (PFA)Push-ups, sit-ups, and a beep test / shuttle run, scored against age- and gender-specific minimums.
  5. InterviewMotivation, values, and role suitability discussion with recruiting staff.
  6. Background & security checksPolice checks and reference checks in both PNG and Australia are likely for this pathway.
  7. Offer & enlistmentRole and initial training location are confirmed on a successful outcome.

Where to watch for updates

Keep an eye on: the PNG Department of Defence and Prime Minister's office announcements, the Australian Department of Defence's ministerial statements, ADF Careers' official site, and reputable PNG news outlets. Avoid third-party sites that claim to have an "application form" for this pathway — as of mid-2026 there is no direct in-country application process confirmed yet.
